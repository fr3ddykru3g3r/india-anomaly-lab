# Questions judges are likely to ask (practise answering without notes)

**Question and design**
1. Why is this a scientific question and not just "finding a profitable strategy"?
2. What are your independent, dependent and control variables?
3. Why pre-register? What would you have done differently if you hadn't?
4. Why a synthetic market? What does it let you know that real data can't?

**Data**
5. How do you know your data includes companies that died? (bhavcopies list what traded each day; E1 counts exits per year)
6. What did you discover about NSE prices on split days, and how did you fix it?
7. Why can't you detect splits from prices alone before 2010? (20% circuit = 1.25 ratio = 1:4 bonus)
8. Why do ISINs change, and how do you link a company's history?
9. Why isn't your universe the Nifty 500?

**Methods**
10. Explain the next-day-open timing. Where could look-ahead bias sneak in, and how does your test catch it?
11. Explain STT, stamp duty and the DP charge. Why does turnover matter so much?
12. How do you model the 2018 LTCG change and grandfathering?
13. What is the Reality Check / SPA test in plain language? Why did you switch to SPA?
14. What does a deflated Sharpe ratio correct for?

**Results and limits**
15. Which hypothesis surprised you most? Which result was negative?
16. Why is equal weight hurt more by survivorship than momentum? (it holds more future failures; momentum rarely holds a falling stock)
17. What are your biggest limitations? (price returns, demergers, daily-data spreads, liquidity universe)
18. What would you do with more time or money?
19. Who could use this? (retail investors, fund-product due diligence, finance educators)

**Process**
20. What did the AI assistant do and what did you do? Show a part of the code you can explain line by line.
